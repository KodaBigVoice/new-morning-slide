# Morning Slide (new version)

Development copy of the Morning Slide. The original lives in the `morningslide` repo and is untouched by work here.

A live form-room morning slide: date, morning task and 8:30 event side by side, "What's happening today?", timetable, today's music lessons and an optional extra panel (house meetings, lunchtime arrangements, or additional information with your own heading for each weekday). Edit on your laptop and press Present to show it on the projector.

Everything you enter is saved in your own browser only. Nothing is uploaded.

## What's new here

- Morning task and 8:30 always sit side by side under the date.
- The timetable can be turned off for any weekday. "What's happening today?" then spreads across both columns.
- The "Additional Information" panel can have its own heading for each weekday (e.g. "Tech Tuesday Task").
- Timetable per weekday: full, first lesson only (shown next to the 8:30 box), or off.
- Two slide designs: Playful (Playpen Sans) and Modern (Bricolage Grotesque, Outfit, Space Mono date).
- Prep slide, switched with the slider at the top left: preps and their minutes per weekday, prep info, tomorrow morning (blank, or copied from tomorrow's morning slide), tomorrow's music lessons, a clock and a prep timer.
- Prep timer: follows each prep's time or chimes every 10 minutes (30 or 40 minute sessions), starts the next timer automatically or waits, plus custom timers. It works from fixed end times, so it doesn't freeze when the tab is in the background.
- Morning prep days (Settings): the prep slide shows today's notes and music lessons instead of tomorrow's.
- The projector shows its own slide: you can edit tomorrow's morning slide while prep is on screen. At 9:00 and 17:30 it offers to move on to the next slide.
