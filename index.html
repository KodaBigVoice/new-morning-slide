House meetings

The school’s schedule is added to your slide automatically, and on these dates the side panel switches to house meetings by itself. You can change any date: your change is kept for that date only, and every other date still gets the school’s updates. Undo a change to go back to the school’s version.

Backup & reset

Everything is automatically saved in this browser, so it’s unlikely you’ll need a backup. You can still save a copy to keep it safe or to move to another device.

Save a copy

Downloads one small file with everything, including all of your settings and preferences.

Keep it in OneDrive and you can open it on any school laptop.

Open a copy

Loads a saved copy, replacing what’s on this laptop. You’ll be asked to confirm first.

Start again

Clears everything in this browser: timetable, notes, preps, design and music lessons. Save a copy first if you might want it back.
