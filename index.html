Start by creating an account

Press Create account at the top right and use your school email. It takes a minute: you’ll get one email to confirm it’s you. Your slide is then saved online, so it’s the same on any laptop you sign in on.

Not ready to create an account? That’s fine: just carry on using Morning Slide as normal.

    Backup & reset

    Everything is automatically saved in this browser, so it’s unlikely you’ll need a backup. You can still save a copy to keep it safe or to move to another device.

    Save a copy

    Downloads one small file with everything, including all of your settings and preferences.

    Keep it in OneDrive and you can open it on any school laptop.

    Open a copy

    Loads a saved copy, replacing what’s on this laptop. You’ll be asked to confirm first.

    Before you start: keeping pupils’ information safe

    Everything in Morning Slide can go up on the projector, and announcements and music lessons are shared with staff across the school.

    • Use pupils’ first names and surname initials only.
    • Never include sensitive information: SEND status, medical, safeguarding, or anything you wouldn’t show the class.
